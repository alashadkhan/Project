# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless
of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Critique the change, not the contributor.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming a motive.
- Accept that "this evidence is thin" is a valid research finding — in the product and in review.
- Respect that this is a solo maintenance effort; responses may take a few days.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or deliberate intimidation.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without explicit permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, reviews or discussions.
- Using the issue tracker to spread misinformation or spam.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and in any space where someone
is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer, Alashad Khan, at alashadkhan.ps9@gmail.com. Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer may warn, edit, lock, or remove content, and may block contributors whose
behaviour is repeated or severe.
